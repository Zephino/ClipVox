PC tool (Windows)

Paste YouTube links, and ClipVox downloads them, removes the music and background noise, and saves TikTrigz-ready clips.

  1. Install Python 3.12 from python.org. On the first install screen, tick “Add python.exe to PATH”.
  2. Download ClipVox with the button at the top of this page, then right-click the zip and choose Extract All…
  3. Double-click install.bat in the extracted folder. The first install downloads about 1 GB and takes a few minutes.
  4. Double-click run.bat, paste YouTube links (one per line), give the voice a name, and click Make clips. Your clips will be in the output folder.

If YouTube downloads stop working, double-click update.bat. You can also run the tool from Command Prompt:

cd %USERPROFILE%\Downloads\ClipVox-main
.venv\Scripts\python.exe clipvox.py --cli "https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID" -n macho_man

Tips for a good voice

Clear, consistent audio matters more than the amount.

Pick talking, not hype

Interviews and sit-down promos work best. Avoid clips with entrance music, crowd noise, or other people talking.

A few minutes is plenty

5 to 15 minutes of clean speech beats an hour of noisy audio. Listen to your clips and delete any bad ones.

Keep it in fun

Use voices for jokes and obvious parody. Label AI voices as AI on TikTok, and don't use them to fool people or make money.
